Uchiage

alt. 「打ち上げ」

noun

u·chi·a·ge uːʧiːɒɡeɪ

  1. A celebratory party held after a successful show¹.
  2. The greatest live North American VTuber/vocaloid/anime music supergroup in history.
  3. The only live North American VTuber/vocaloid/anime music supergroup in history.
¹In the context of performing arts in Japanese culture.

Known for their massively successful debut live performances at SURGE! 3D Live Musical and OffKai Relay: Carousel Concert, fan-favorite supergroup Uchiage sees absolutely no limits to their potential. Established in 2026, the group formed after two OffKai Expo staff members (who are now members of the band) had a few drinks and then decided to pitch the idea of 'live band plus Vtuber vocalist' to the OffKai upper brass. Uchiage synthesizes the talents and breadth of musical expertise of the instrumentalists from multiple pre-established North American music groups. Petty Tactic. Project Mikan. Aimai Mirai. And more. Fans from all over the world can't wait to see which direction Uchiage will launch off to next.
