Known for their successful debut performances at SURGE! 3D Live Musical and OffKai Relay: Carousel Concert, live band collaboration project Uchiage has become a fan-favorite in no time at all. Established in 2026, the group formed after two OffKai Expo staff members (who are now members of the band) had a few drinks and then decided to pitch the idea of "live band plus Vtuber vocalist" to the OffKai upper brass. Uchiage synthesizes the talents and breadth of musical expertise of instrumentalists from multiple pre-established American music groups. Petty Tactic. Project Mikan. Aimai Mirai. And more. Fans from all over the world can't wait to see the places Uchiage will launch off to next.

Book Uchiage for your next con or other live project. should I mention we'll hop on someone's covers or originals too if they want a band to record something?
